Target audience segments — app downloads focus

New homeowner
Griha Pravesh

Urban, 28–45, just bought or moving into a new home. High-intent trigger with a clear date. Primary puja type. Lives in Tier 1/2 cities, discovers via search.

Reach via → Real estate platforms, Meta life-event targeting, Google search

Wedding family
Marriage Puja

Parents/family planning a wedding, 40–60. Highest average order value. Traditional, values authenticity and language-specific pandits. Plans months ahead.

Reach via → Wedding platforms, wedding venue tie-ups, festive season ads

Festival devotee
High volume

25–55, celebrates all major Hindu festivals. Wants convenience for Diwali, Navratri, Ganesh Chaturthi, Shravan Pujas. High frequency, moderate order value.

Reach via → Festival-triggered campaigns, devotional content, YouTube

NRI diaspora
High value

25–50, living in USA, UK, UAE, Singapore. High disposable income. Wants E-Puja or pujas arranged in India for family. Pays premium for reliability.

Reach via → NRI community groups, Instagram diaspora targeting, YouTube

New parent
Birth ceremonies

25–40, planning Namkaran, Mundan, Annaprashan. Predictable timing. Millennial parents, comfortable booking digitally. Strong word-of-mouth potential.

Reach via → Baby/parenting apps, mom communities, hospital tie-ups

Dosha remedy seeker
Repeat user

30–55, astrology-driven. Books Kaal Sarp Dosh, Mangal Dosh, Pitru Dosh pujas. Referred by astrologers. High intent, consistent demand year-round.

Reach via → Astrology content, influencer partnerships, search ads

Online — acquisition + retention

Meta App Install Campaigns

Priority 1

Life-event targeting (new home, new baby, marriage engagement). Festival-triggered bursts for Navratri, Shravan, Diwali. Lookalike audiences from existing bookers. Retargeting web visitors who bounced without downloading.

Google UAC (App campaigns)

Priority 1

"Pandit near me", "Griha Pravesh puja", "book pandit online" queries → direct app install. YouTube pre-roll ads before devotional, bhajan, and astrology content. High-intent search-to-download funnel.

Reels & short video content

Organic growth

Daily muhurat/panchang reels. "How this puja is performed" education series. Pandit spotlight stories. Behind-the-scenes of a ceremony. Festival countdown content. Each reel ends with an app download CTA.

WhatsApp Business broadcasts

Retention

Festival reminder messages 7 days before major pujas. Shubh Muhurat alerts for the week. Post-booking updates. Re-engagement campaigns for users who booked 6+ months ago. Direct link to app booking.

Email & retention funnels

Retention

Post-booking: ritual guide PDF → review request → next puja suggestion. Annual reminders (Varshika Shradh anniversary pujas). Festival calendar drip campaign. App re-engagement for inactive users.

Influencer & creator partnerships

Trust building

Spiritual influencers (100K–1M followers). Astrology content creators — huge, loyal audiences. Mom/parenting bloggers for birth ceremony content. Home & interior influencers for Griha Pravesh. Authentic UGC-style placements, not scripted ads.

SEO → app conversion bridge

Quick win

99Pandit already ranks for thousands of keywords. The gap: no app download CTAs on high-traffic pages. Add smart banners, pop-ups, and inline CTAs on the top 50 pages. Also: App Store Optimisation (ASO) for discoverability.

Offline — on-ground acquisition

Temple QR activations

High reach

QR code standees and posters at major temples in Varanasi, Ujjain, Haridwar, Tirupati, Shirdi. Placed at prasad counters and notice boards. "Don't have a pandit yet? Book in 2 minutes" messaging. Pilgrimage visitors = high intent.

Festival activations

Seasonal

Community puja sponsorship during Navratri, Ganesh Chaturthi, Diwali — branded as "presented by 99Pandit". Live stream free group pujas to showcase E-Puja. On-ground teams with app download assistance. Strong PR hook.

Real estate developer tie-ups

Best ROI offline

Partner with major builders — Sobha, Prestige, Godrej, DLF, Lodha. 99Pandit QR on the key handover kit / welcome pack. "Your new home deserves a blessed beginning" — the Griha Pravesh puja conversion rate on new handovers would be extraordinary.

RWA / housing society partnerships

Ongoing

Partner with Resident Welfare Associations in large apartment complexes. Flyers in lift lobbies, society noticeboards. Offer a "society exclusive" first-puja discount. Societies with active community groups are ideal word-of-mouth engines.

Wedding venue partnerships

Year-round

Banquet halls, marriage gardens, wedding planners become referral partners. Mandap decorators, catering companies, venue coordinators — all recommended vendors who can suggest 99Pandit for the religious ceremony component.

Samagri shop placements

Low cost

QR code stickers and small standees at puja item shops, agarbatti counters, and religious stationery stores. Someone buying samagri is actively planning a puja. High intent at the point of purchase.

Cross-promotional partnerships

Real estate platforms

Biggest opportunity

NoBroker, 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com. "Moving in? Book your Griha Pravesh Puja" prompt after lease/purchase confirmation. Co-marketing during new project launches. Potential deep-link integration.

Wedding platforms

Seasonal volume

WedMeGood, ShaadiSaga, Wedbook, WeddingWire India. 99Pandit listed as the go-to puja booking platform in their vendor marketplace. Co-campaigns during peak wedding seasons (Nov–Feb, Apr–May).

Baby & parenting platforms

Niche, high-trust

BabyChakra, Healofy, mom Facebook groups. Promote Namkaran, Mundan, Annaprashan booking. New parents are among the highest-converting and most word-of-mouth-active customers.

Devotional OTT & TV

Mass reach

Sadhana TV, Aastha, Bhakti channels — pre-roll or mid-roll ad placements before bhajan/katha content. The audience overlap is near-perfect. Also: sponsorships for popular bhajan YouTube channels.

NRI community channels

High value, low cost

Indian diaspora Facebook groups (USA/UK/UAE), NRI WhatsApp communities, Indian cultural organisations abroad. E-Puja is the perfect product here. Partner with Desi community event organisers for visibility.

Spiritual tourism operators

Premium niche

Varanasi, Ujjain, Haridwar, Tirupati tour packages. Travel operators who take groups for Char Dham or jyotirlinga circuits. Bundle 99Pandit's pandit booking into the itinerary — especially for NRIs doing India pilgrimages.
